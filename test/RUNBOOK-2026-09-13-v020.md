opposite responses.

## Part B — the HW-V6-01 measurement

This is the row the response-header oracle **cannot** run. `test/oracle/` is
same-origin by ruling, so initiator equals target there and every row passes
whether or not HeaderWright models initiators at all.

### B1 — cross-origin, only the target granted

Confirm `nothw.test` is NOT granted (no chip for it; check
`chrome://extensions` → Details → Site access).

Open `http://nothw.test:8790/`, press **Measure**.

**Predicted:** verdict reads CROSS-ORIGIN with initiator origin
`http://nothw.test:8790`; **`x-hw-probe: ABSENT`**.

This is HW-V6-01. Chrome requires host permission for the request URL *and* the
initiator for everything except navigations; only the target is granted.

**Observed:** ______________________________________________

> **A failed fetch is NOT this result.** The page renders MEASUREMENT FAILED in
> red for that case. Record it as not-run, not as absent — reading a failed
> fetch as a confirmation would manufacture exactly the finding being tested.

### B2 — grant the initiator too

Add `nothw.test` to the `probe` profile's domains. Grant when prompted.
Re-run `http://nothw.test:8790/` → **Measure**.

**Predicted:** `x-hw-probe: "present"`. Both halves now granted.

**Observed:** ______________________________________________

> B1 absent + B2 present is the decisive pair. **Either result on its own is
> weak**: B1 alone could be any failure; B2 alone could be a same-origin path.

### B3 — does the grant survive a revoke?

Remove `nothw.test` from the profile. Re-run.

**Predicted:** back to `x-hw-probe: ABSENT`.

**Observed:** ______________________________________________

### B4 — what the popup claims during B1

While in the B1 state, hover the `hw.test` chip.

**Predicted:** tooltip reads *access granted. Headers apply to page loads here,
and to requests made by pages on this or another granted domain.* Green dot.
